# Reg Z (Truth in Lending) — 12 CFR Part 1026

## Overview

Reg Z implements the Truth in Lending Act (TILA). It requires creditors to disclose the cost of credit to consumers and provides protections for credit transactions. Covers open-end credit (credit cards, HELOCs), closed-end credit (auto loans, personal loans), mortgage transactions (TRID, ATR/QM, HOEPA), and private education loans. Enforced by the CFPB. One of the most complex consumer financial regulations.

## Structure & Key Sections

### Subpart A — General (1026.1–1026.4)

#### 1026.1 — Authority, Purpose, Coverage
Applies to creditors who regularly extend consumer credit subject to a finance charge or payable in more than four installments. Exemptions for business/commercial/agricultural credit above certain thresholds.

#### 1026.2 — Definitions
Key terms: creditor, finance charge, annual percentage rate (APR), open-end credit, closed-end credit, consummation, rescission, dwelling.

#### 1026.3 — Exempt Transactions
Exemptions: business/commercial/agricultural (over threshold), government entity credit, securities credit, certain employer plans.

#### 1026.4 — Finance Charge
What is and is not included in the finance charge. Critical for APR calculation. Exclusions: application fees, late fees, overdraft fees, seller's points, certain insurance premiums.

### Subpart B — Open-End Credit (1026.5–1026.16)

#### 1026.5 — General Disclosure Requirements
Timing and format. Initial disclosures before first transaction. Minimum 21-day grace period for purchases if one is offered.

#### 1026.6 — Account-Opening Disclosures (Schumer Box)
Tabular format ("Schumer box"): APR (purchase, balance transfer, cash advance, penalty), fee schedule (annual, transaction, penalty, late), grace period, balance computation method, billing rights summary.

#### 1026.7 — Periodic Statement
Required content: previous balance, charges/credits during period, periodic rate and APR, finance charge, new balance, minimum payment, late payment warning, minimum payment warning.

#### 1026.9 — Subsequent Disclosures
Change-in-terms: 45 days advance notice for significant changes to open-end credit (rate increases, fee changes). Right to reject and close account.

#### 1026.10 — Payments
Payment allocation rules: amounts above minimum payment allocated to highest-rate balance first. Must credit payment as of date received if received by 5 PM cutoff.

#### 1026.12 — Special Credit Card Provisions
Unauthorized use liability: **$50 maximum**, regardless of timing (contrast with Reg E tiered liability). Issuer burden of proof. Card must be an accepted credit card. Rights of card issuer in asserting claims against cardholder.

#### 1026.13 — Billing Error Resolution (FCBA)
Error types: unauthorized charges, charges for goods not received, computational errors, failure to credit payments, charges needing clarification.
**Consumer notice**: written, within 60 days of statement.
**Investigation**: creditor must acknowledge within 30 days, resolve within 2 billing cycles (maximum 90 days).
Must credit disputed amount during investigation; may not restrict account or report as delinquent.

#### 1026.14 — APR Determination (Open-End)
Methods for computing APR for periodic statements.

#### 1026.15 — Right of Rescission (HELOCs)
3-business-day rescission right for HELOCs secured by principal dwelling. Does not apply to initial purchase-money mortgages.

#### 1026.16 — Advertising (Open-End)
Triggered terms: if APR or finance charge is stated, additional terms must be disclosed. "Introductory rate" advertising rules.

### Subpart C — Closed-End Credit (1026.17–1026.24)

#### 1026.17 — General Disclosure Requirements
Disclosures must be provided before consummation. Clear and conspicuous, grouped together, in writing.

#### 1026.18 — Content of Disclosures
Key disclosures: creditor identity, amount financed, itemization of amount financed, finance charge, APR, variable rate information, payment schedule, total of payments, total interest percentage, late payment fee.

#### 1026.19 — Certain Mortgage Disclosures (TRID)
**Loan Estimate (LE)**: provided within 3 business days of application. Itemizes estimated loan costs.
**Closing Disclosure (CD)**: provided at least 3 business days before consummation. Replaces HUD-1.
Revised LE: permitted only for valid changed circumstances.
TRID tolerance rules: 0% tolerance (fees creditor controls), 10% tolerance (third-party fees with selected provider), no tolerance limit (government recording fees, prepaids).

#### 1026.20 — Subsequent Events
Refinancing disclosure requirements. ARM adjustment notices: 60-210 days before first adjusted payment; 25-120 days for subsequent adjustments.

#### 1026.22 — APR Determination (Closed-End)
APR calculation methods for closed-end credit. Irregular transactions. Accuracy tolerances.

#### 1026.23 — Right of Rescission (Closed-End Mortgages)
3-business-day rescission right for certain closed-end mortgages secured by principal dwelling. Does NOT apply to purchase-money mortgages. Extended rescission (up to 3 years) if material disclosures not provided.

#### 1026.24 — Advertising (Closed-End)
Triggered terms: if any of rate, payment, downpayment, or finance charge is stated, all must be stated. Must use "annual percentage rate" (full term).

### Subpart E — Special Rules for Mortgage Transactions (1026.31–1026.43)

#### 1026.32 — High-Cost Mortgages (HOEPA)
Thresholds for "high-cost mortgage" designation: APR exceeds APOR by specified amount, points/fees exceed specified percentage of loan amount, or prepayment penalty period exceeds 36 months. Additional protections: prohibited terms, counseling requirement, homeownership counseling referral.

#### 1026.34 — Prohibited Acts for High-Cost Mortgages
No balloon payments, no negative amortization, no prepayment penalties, no financing of points/fees, no due-on-demand, no modification/deferral fees.

#### 1026.35 — Higher-Priced Mortgage Loans (HPML)
APR exceeds APOR by 1.5% (first lien) or 3.5% (subordinate). Requires escrow account (at least 5 years), appraisal requirements.

#### 1026.36 — Loan Originator Compensation
Prohibits compensation based on transaction terms (other than loan amount). Dual compensation prohibition. Qualification requirements for originators.

#### 1026.37 — Loan Estimate Content
Detailed requirements for LE form content: loan terms, projected payments, costs at closing, loan costs (origination charges, services), other costs (taxes, prepaids, escrow), cash to close.

#### 1026.38 — Closing Disclosure Content
Detailed requirements for CD form content: mirrors LE with actual figures, adds seller information, summaries of transactions, contact information.

#### 1026.39 — Mortgage Transfer Disclosures
30 calendar days notice when mortgage loan is sold or transferred.

#### 1026.41 — Periodic Statements for Residential Mortgage Loans
Required content: amount due, payment breakdown (principal, interest, escrow), transaction activity, partial payment information, delinquency information, loss mitigation language.

#### 1026.43 — Ability-to-Repay / Qualified Mortgage (ATR/QM)
**ATR**: creditor must make reasonable, good-faith determination of ability to repay. Must consider 8 underwriting factors (income, employment, payment on covered transaction, payment on simultaneous loans, monthly obligations, DTI, credit history, remaining mortgage amount).
**QM**: safe harbor/rebuttable presumption of ATR compliance. General QM: DTI limit based on price-based approach (APR vs APOR spread). No risky features (interest-only, negative amortization, balloon, term >30 years, excessive points/fees).

### Subpart F — Private Education Loans (1026.46–1026.48)
Disclosure and right-to-cancel requirements for private education loans.

### Subpart G — Credit Card Rules (1026.51–1026.61)

#### 1026.51 — Ability to Pay (CARD Act)
Card issuer must consider ability to make required minimum payments. Under-21 requirements: independent ability to pay or cosigner.

#### 1026.52 — Fee Limitations
Fees in first year cannot exceed 25% of initial credit limit. Late payment fees: safe harbor amounts (adjusted annually).

#### 1026.53 — Allocation of Payments
Payments exceeding minimum must be allocated to highest-rate balance first (with limited exceptions).

#### 1026.55 — Limitations on Interest Rate Increases
Cannot increase rate in first year (with exceptions). Must provide 45-day advance notice. Must reevaluate rate increases every 6 months.

#### 1026.56 — Overlimit
Cannot impose over-limit fee unless consumer opts in. May not charge more than one fee per billing cycle.

#### 1026.57 — College Student Card Marketing
Restrictions on card marketing on or near college campuses.

## Key Thresholds & Timelines

- **$50**: maximum unauthorized credit card use liability (1026.12)
- **3 business days**: Loan Estimate delivery after application (1026.19)
- **3 business days**: Closing Disclosure delivery before consummation (1026.19)
- **21 days**: minimum grace period for purchases (1026.5(b)(2)(ii))
- **30 days**: creditor must acknowledge billing error (1026.13)
- **2 billing cycles (max 90 days)**: billing error resolution deadline (1026.13)
- **45 days**: advance notice for open-end credit changes (1026.9)
- **30 calendar days**: mortgage transfer notice (1026.39)
- **6 months**: rate increase reevaluation period (1026.55)
- **25%**: first-year fee cap as percentage of credit limit (1026.52)
- **0%/10%/unlimited**: TRID tolerance categories (1026.19(e))
