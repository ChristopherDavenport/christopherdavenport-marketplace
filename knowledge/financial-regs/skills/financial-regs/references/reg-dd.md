# Reg DD (Truth in Savings) — 12 CFR Part 1030

## Overview

Reg DD implements the Truth in Savings Act. It requires depository institutions to disclose interest rates, fees, and terms on deposit accounts so consumers can make informed comparisons. Covers savings, checking, money market, and CD accounts. Enforced by the CFPB.

## CFR Citation

12 CFR Part 1030 (Title 12, Chapter X, Part 1030)

## Structure & Key Sections

### 1030.1 — Authority, Purpose, Coverage
Scope: applies to depository institutions that offer deposit accounts to consumers. Does not apply to credit unions regulated under NCUA Part 707.

### 1030.2 — Definitions
Key terms: annual percentage yield (APY), account, bonus, deposit broker, fixed-rate account, grace period, interest rate, stepped-rate account, tiered-rate account, variable-rate account.

### 1030.3 — General Disclosure Requirements
Form requirements: clear and conspicuous, in writing (or electronic under E-SIGN), in a form the consumer can keep. Disclosures may be provided in any format that groups related information together.

### 1030.4 — Account Disclosures (MOST REFERENCED)
What must be disclosed before account opening: rate information (APY, interest rate, compounding/crediting frequency, variable-rate mechanism), minimum balance requirements (to open, earn APY, avoid fees), fees (maintenance, activity, penalty), transaction limitations, features (maturity date for CDs, early withdrawal penalties, renewal policy).

### 1030.5 — Subsequent Disclosures
Change-in-terms: 30 calendar days advance notice before any change that may reduce APY or adversely affect the consumer. No notice required for variable-rate changes tied to an index.

### 1030.6 — Periodic Statement Disclosures
Required on periodic statements: APY earned, amount of interest earned, fees imposed (itemized), length of statement period, aggregate year-to-date fee totals.

### 1030.7 — Payment of Interest
Rules on when interest must accrue on noncash deposits and the balance computation methods institutions may use.

### 1030.8 — Advertising
Advertising rules: no misleading or inaccurate representations. Triggered terms (if APY is stated, must also state certain other terms). Indoor signs, outdoor signs, broadcast, electronic media, and print each have specific rules. Bonus requirements: must state minimum balance and time requirements.

### 1030.9 — Enforcement and Record Retention
2-year record retention for evidence of compliance.

### 1030.11 — Overdraft Services
Additional disclosure requirements for institutions advertising or promoting overdraft coverage programs. Aggregate overdraft/returned-item fee totals required on periodic statements (year-to-date and for statement period).

### Appendix A — APY Calculation
Formulas for calculating APY and annual percentage yield earned. Covers simple interest, compounding, tiered rates, stepped rates, and 365 vs 366 day year.

### Appendix B — Model Clauses and Sample Forms
Safe harbor model forms for account disclosures. Using model clauses provides compliance protection.

## Key Thresholds & Timelines

- **30 calendar days**: advance notice for adverse change in terms (1030.5)
- **2 years**: record retention period (1030.9)
- **0.01%**: APY rounding precision (Appendix A)
- **365 days**: base year for APY calculation (366 in leap year for daily accrual)
