# FI Operations — Canonical Journal Entries

## Overview

This is the **lean** companion to the structural references. It catalogs the canonical journal entries for the major FI operations (loans, deposits, securities, ALLL/CECL, fee income), shows which sub-ledger holds the underlying detail, identifies the GL control account, and points to the relevant Call Report line and FASB ASC topic.

This reference is illustrative — institution-specific COAs, products, and core platforms produce variations. For standards detail (when to recognize, how to measure), see [fasb-asc.md](fasb-asc.md). For the structural concepts behind these entries, see [journals.md](journals.md), [ledgers.md](ledgers.md), and [sub-ledgers.md](sub-ledgers.md).

## Citation

GAAP basis: ASC 310 (Receivables), ASC 320 (Debt Securities), ASC 326 (Credit Losses), ASC 815 (Derivatives), ASC 825 (Financial Instruments), ASC 942 (Depository and Lending). Call Report basis: FFIEC 031/041/051.

## Structure & Key Sections

### 1. Loans

#### Loan origination — funding

```
Date         Account                                     Debit       Credit
2026-05-04   130500  Loans — C&I                       250,000.00
             140000      Allowance for Credit Losses                  3,000.00   (Day-1 CECL)
             100200      Cash — Federal Reserve                     247,000.00
             To fund loan #LN-12345 to Acme LLC; CECL day-1 reserve 1.2%
```

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Sub-ledger | Loan servicing system, by loan # |
| GL control account | 130500 — Loans — C&I |
| Call Report line | RC-C item 4 |
| ASC | ASC 310 (origination), ASC 326-20 (day-1 CECL) |

Note: Origination fees and direct origination costs are deferred and amortized over loan life as a yield adjustment under **ASC 310-20** (effective interest method). They are netted into the loan carrying value via account 139000.

#### Loan principal payment

```
2026-06-01   100200  Cash — Federal Reserve              5,200.00
             130500      Loans — C&I                                   5,000.00
             180100      Accrued Interest Receivable — Loans             200.00
             Principal $5,000 + interest $200 received on loan #LN-12345
```

In practice, separate principal/interest split is generated by the loan servicing system based on the amortization schedule.

#### Interest accrual (daily or monthly)

```
2026-05-31   180100  Accrued Interest Receivable — Loans  1,250.00
             400100      Interest Income — Loans                       1,250.00
             Daily/monthly accrual on commercial loan portfolio
```

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Sub-ledger | Per-loan accrual record |
| GL control | 180100 — Accrued Interest Receivable — Loans |
| Call Report line | Other Assets — RC item 11 |
| ASC | ASC 310 (recognition); nonaccrual policy per FFIEC instructions |

**Nonaccrual**: When a loan goes 90 days past due (or sooner if collection is doubtful), interest accrual stops and previously accrued but uncollected interest is reversed:

```
2026-08-15   400100  Interest Income — Loans              3,500.00
             180100      Accrued Interest Receivable — Loans          3,500.00
             Reverse accrued interest on nonaccrual loan #LN-12345
```

#### Charge-off

```
2026-12-15   140000  Allowance for Credit Losses        225,000.00
             130500      Loans — C&I                                 225,000.00
             Charge off loan #LN-12345; balance unrecoverable
```

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Sub-ledger | Loan record marked charged-off; retained for recovery tracking |
| GL effect | Reduces both the loan asset and the allowance — no P&L impact at charge-off |
| Call Report | RI-B item 1 (charge-offs) |
| ASC | ASC 326-20-35 (writeoff when uncollectible) |

#### Recovery (post-chargeoff cash received)

```
2027-03-10   100200  Cash — Federal Reserve              50,000.00
             140000      Allowance for Credit Losses                  50,000.00
             Recovery on previously charged-off loan #LN-12345
```

Recoveries replenish the allowance (no P&L impact directly; net charge-off = charge-offs − recoveries reduces required provision).

### 2. Deposits

#### Account opening with initial deposit

```
2026-05-04   100100  Cash on hand                       1,000.00
             200100      Demand Deposits — non-interest                1,000.00
             Open DDA account #1234567890 for new customer
```

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Sub-ledger | DDA system, account #1234567890 |
| GL control | 200100 — Demand Deposits |
| Call Report line | RC-E item 1.a |

#### Customer withdrawal

```
2026-05-15   200100  Demand Deposits — non-interest      500.00
             100100      Cash on hand                                  500.00
             Withdrawal from account #1234567890
```

#### Interest credit on savings/MMDA

```
2026-05-31   500200  Interest Expense — Savings/MMDA   42,150.00
             280100      Accrued Interest Payable — Deposits         42,150.00
             Monthly interest accrual on savings portfolio

2026-06-01   280100  Accrued Interest Payable — Deposits 42,150.00
             201100      Savings Deposits                             42,150.00
             Credit interest to customer accounts
```

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Sub-ledger | Per-account accrual; per-account credit |
| GL control | 280100 (accrual) → 201100 (credit) |
| Call Report | Interest expense RI item 2.a; deposits RC-E item 4 |

#### NSF / overdraft fee

```
2026-05-20   200100  Demand Deposits — non-interest      35.00
             700100      Service Charges on Deposit Accounts            35.00
             NSF fee on returned item, account #1234567890
```

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Sub-ledger | DDA — fee posting on customer account |
| GL control | 200100 (deposit reduced) → 700100 (income) |
| Call Report line | RI item 5.b |
| Cross-reference | Reg E and Reg DD constrain disclosure and opt-in for overdraft programs — see `financial-regs` plugin |

### 3. Investment Securities

Classification at acquisition determines accounting treatment for the life of the security (ASC 320, ASC 326-30 for HTM credit losses).

| Classification | Measurement | Unrealized G/L Goes To | ASC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Held-to-Maturity (HTM) | Amortized cost | Not recognized; FV disclosed | ASC 320-10-25-1 |
| Available-for-Sale (AFS) | Fair value | OCI (net of tax) | ASC 320-10-35-1 |
| Trading | Fair value | Earnings (P&L) | ASC 320-10-35-1 |

#### Securities purchase — AFS

```
2026-05-04   111100  Investment Securities — AFS      1,002,500.00
             100200      Cash — Federal Reserve                     1,002,500.00
             Purchase $1M par U.S. Treasury at 100.25; CUSIP 912828XXX
```

#### AFS fair value mark (period-end)

```
2026-06-30   111100  Investment Securities — AFS         12,500.00
             380100      AOCI — Unrealized G/L on AFS                 9,375.00
             280500      Deferred tax liability                       3,125.00
             Mark AFS portfolio to fair value; gain $12,500 (25% tax)
```

For a loss, the entry direction reverses; AFS losses also require credit-loss assessment under **ASC 326-30** (replaces prior OTTI guidance under ASC 320-10-35).

#### Securities interest accrual + premium amortization

```
2026-05-31   180200  Accrued Interest Receivable — Securities  3,750.00
             111100      Investment Securities — AFS (premium amort)    250.00
             410100      Interest Income — Securities — taxable        4,000.00
             Monthly accrual + premium amortization on AFS portfolio
```

The premium/discount amortization uses the effective interest method per **ASC 310-20** and is reflected as a yield adjustment.

### 4. Allowance for Credit Losses (ALLL → ACL under CECL)

Under CECL (**ASC 326-20**, effective for SEC filers in 2020 and all other entities in 2023), the allowance reflects expected lifetime credit losses, not incurred losses.

#### Initial CECL adoption (one-time, historical — included for context)

```
20XX-01-01   350100  Retained Earnings                  X,XXX,XXX
             140000      Allowance for Credit Losses                  X,XXX,XXX
             CECL adoption — increase allowance via cumulative-effect adjustment to RE
             (per ASC 326-10-65-1, net of tax)
```

#### Period-end provision (recurring)

```
2026-06-30   600100  Provision for Credit Losses        450,000.00
             140000      Allowance for Credit Losses                 450,000.00
             Q2 CECL provision based on model output
```

If the model indicates a *reduction* in required allowance (improved economic outlook, paydowns), the entry reverses (negative provision = "release").

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Sub-ledger | CECL model output, by pool/segment/vintage |
| GL control | 140000 — ACL — Loans (contra-asset) |
| Call Report | RI item 4 (provision); RC item 4.c (allowance, negative) |
| ASC | ASC 326-20 (loans/HTM), ASC 326-30 (AFS) |

CECL also applies to:
- Off-balance-sheet credit exposures (unfunded commitments) — allowance is a liability (account 280900-range), provision per **ASC 326-20-30-11**
- HTM debt securities (account 141000)
- AFS debt securities — limited to amount fair value is below amortized cost, recognized as a separate allowance, not OCI (**ASC 326-30**)

### 5. Fee Income (selected)

Most non-interest fee revenue is recognized under **ASC 606** (Revenue from Contracts with Customers), but several FI fee streams have specific guidance:

| Fee Stream | Recognition Basis | ASC | Typical Entry |
|---|---|---|---|
| NSF / overdraft fees | Point in time (transaction occurrence) | ASC 606 / institution policy | See "Deposits — NSF" above |
| Account analysis / monthly maintenance | Over time (period in which service rendered) | ASC 606 | Dr DDA, Cr 700100 monthly |
| Loan origination fees | Deferred and amortized over loan life as yield adjustment | **ASC 310-20** (NOT ASC 606) | Dr Loan, Cr 139000 (deferred); amortized into 400100 |
| Loan commitment fees | Depends on likelihood of drawdown — if remote, recognize over commitment period; if not remote, defer per ASC 310-20 | ASC 310-20-25-19 | Variable |
| Servicing fees (loans serviced for others) | Over time as service provided | ASC 860 (servicing assets) and ASC 606 | Dr Cash, Cr 740100 |
| Trust fiduciary fees | Over time | ASC 606 | Dr Cash, Cr 710100 |
| Interchange revenue | Point in time (per transaction) | ASC 606 | Dr Cash, Cr 770100 |

**Important divergence**: Loan origination fees follow **ASC 310-20**, not ASC 606. This is a frequent confusion — fees that compensate the lender for originating the loan are part of the loan's effective yield.
